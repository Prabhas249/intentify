# Intentify — Strategy & Positioning

> The north star. Every feature, page, and marketing decision gets checked against this doc.
> Last updated: 2026-06-05

---

## The one sentence

**Intentify is the conversion engine for online stores: it sees every visitor's intent and source, catches the hot ones with the right popup at the right moment, keeps them as a lead, and tells you exactly which channel made the money.**

Pitch line: **"DataFast tells you they left. We bring them back."**

---

## The market reality (why this works)

### The competitors are all the same tool

DataFast, Sleek, and Piqo are **one product wearing three logos**: privacy-first analytics + Stripe revenue attribution + a live visitor globe. They're converging on identical features and racing each other to the bottom on price.

| | DataFast | Piqo | Sleek |
|---|---|---|---|
| Core | Privacy analytics + revenue | Same + aggregation | Same + AI chat |
| Live globe | ✅ | ✅ | ✅ |
| Revenue attribution | Stripe, LS, Polar, Shopify | Stripe, Polar, Paddle, **Dodo**, Creem | Stripe |
| Extras | AI agent / CLI | Search Console, signups, affiliate links | AI chat, web vitals |
| Price | $9–19/mo | **$5.75/mo** (annual) | $9–19/mo |
| Real moat | **Marc Lou's 127k audience + ShipFast** | low price | — |

### The trap to avoid

DataFast has 18,000+ users **because Marc Lou has 127,000 Twitter followers and an ecosystem (ShipFast/CodeFast)** — not because the product is unbeatable. **His moat is distribution, not features.**

> ❌ Do NOT try to out-feature DataFast and win his customers. That fight is distribution-gated and unwinnable. You'd build a better product with zero audience.

### The opening they leave wide open

**All three tools STOP at "here's who's high-intent" — and then do nothing.** They are a *mirror*. They show you the sale leaving and never lift a finger.

That gap is the whole company.

## The wedge: analytics that ACTS

The action layer (intent → popup → capture → WhatsApp) is **near-useless to DataFast's audience (SaaS founders) and ESSENTIAL to ours (stores).**

- A SaaS founder wants a clean dashboard.
- A Shopify/D2C store **lives on popups** — cart recovery, discount capture, email/WhatsApp lists.
- **A popup is worth 10x more to a store than to a SaaS app.**

So analytics + popup + capture is **not "a better DataFast." It's a different animal: the conversion engine for stores.** DataFast can't follow us there — his audience doesn't want popups and his brand is "simple SaaS analytics."

### Category framing

| Them | Us |
|------|-----|
| **Passive analytics** | **Active analytics** |
| "See where your money came from" | "See it, then make more of it happen" |
| A mirror | A salesperson |

## The loop (the product, in 4 words)

```
   SEE   →   ACT   →   KEEP   →   ATTRIBUTE
 analytics  popup    capture    revenue
  "who's    "catch   "save the   "which
   hot"     them"    lead"       rupee"
```

- **They** own SEE and ATTRIBUTE (analytics + Stripe revenue).
- **We** own ACT and KEEP — **the middle, where money actually changes hands.** Plus SEE and ATTRIBUTE, because we already built them.

**Discipline rule:** the product is this loop. The day it becomes "analytics AND popups AND capture AND email AND CRM AND…" we've lost. Everything must serve See → Act → Keep → Attribute.

## Beachhead: who we sell to first

**Indian D2C / Shopify / ecommerce stores.** Not global indie SaaS founders.

Why:
- It's where the action layer (popup/WhatsApp/capture) is worth the most.
- It's where DataFast/Piqo/Sleek don't compete and Marc Lou's audience doesn't help him.
- WhatsApp + INR + D2C is an unfair advantage none of them have.

Expand to "global stores" later. **Win one beach before opening a second front.**

## What to steal from each

- **From DataFast** → revenue attribution + the **addictive live globe** (people leave it open on a second monitor — that emotional dopamine pull *is* a growth loop) + radical simplicity + the AI/CLI angle.
- **From Piqo** → "one tool instead of five" aggregation, **Dodo Payments** (already our choice), affiliate links, and their ~$5.75/mo as the price *floor we never go below*.
- **From Sleek** → "ask your data a question" AI chat.
- **From us (what none of them have)** → intent scoring, popup engine, lead capture, WhatsApp. The unfair half.

---

## Pricing principle

**Do not be the cheap one.** Cheap fights our own revenue target (more customers, more churn, more support for the same MRR) and we can't out-cheap a solo founder with no payroll.

- Win on the action layer ("it pays for itself because it sells"), not on price.
- A tool that recovers even one lost sale justifies its cost — that's a *value* story, not a *discount* story.
- Keep INR pricing for India (the local-affordability edge) and USD for the rest — but priced on **value**, not undercutting.

## Honest status (2026-06-05)

**What's NOT the gap:** product. ~95% of the MVP is built — analytics (globe, tracking, UTM, revenue) **and** the action layer (intent, popup, capture, WhatsApp). We already have **more built than Piqo sells.**

**What IS the gap:**
1. **Billing** — Dodo Payments not wired up. Nobody can pay yet. ← #1 blocker.
2. **Plan enforcement** — visitor limits not enforced.
3. **Distribution** — no audience, no users. The real long game.
4. **Polish** — script minification (21KB → 5KB), onboarding, the addictive live-globe moment.

**The move:** build *less*, ship, get **5 real Shopify/D2C stores** using it, let them tell us what to add next.

---

## Decision filter (pin this)

Before building anything, ask:
1. Does it serve **See → Act → Keep → Attribute**? If no, cut it.
2. Does it help a **store sell more**, or just look nice? Sell wins.
3. Does it move us toward **first paying customer**, or is it a distraction from billing/distribution?
